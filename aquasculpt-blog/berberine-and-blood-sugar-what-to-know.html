Berberine and Blood Sugar: What To Know

Why it is studied and why it needs care.

Berberine is a yellow alkaloid found in several plants. See safety and interactions.

Why It Is Studied

Trials suggest it can lower blood sugar modestly. Read the ingredients.

Why That Matters

If you take diabetes medicine, an added effect could push blood sugar too low. Read the ingredients.

What To Do

Ask your doctor and pharmacist before starting. Read the ingredients.
