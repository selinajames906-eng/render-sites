Vitamin B6 And B Vitamin Limits

More is not always better with B vitamins.*

The B6 Catch

Vitamin B6 is needed by nerves, yet long-term high intakes, from supplements, can themselves cause nerve damage with numbness and tingling. The amount in this liquid is not stated.

See also: Find the cause first · Medicines and allergies

Add Up What You Take

Many multivitamins, B-complexes, energy drinks and other nerve products contain B6, so totals can climb. Check every label.

See also: Medicines and allergies · FAQ

B12 Is Different

Low B12 is a proven cause of neuropathy, and a blood test can show it. Treatment for a deficiency is dosed by a doctor. See B vitamins and nerve health.
