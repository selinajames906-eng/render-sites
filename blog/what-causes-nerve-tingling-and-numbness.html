What Causes Nerve Tingling And Numbness

Tingling and numbness are signals, not a diagnosis.

Common Causes

Sitting or sleeping on a nerve, carpal tunnel or a pinched nerve in the neck or back, diabetes, low vitamin B12, alcohol, some medicines and thyroid or kidney problems.
