Peripheral Neuropathy: Basics And Treatment

Peripheral neuropathy is damage or dysfunction of the nerves outside the brain and spinal cord.

Treatment

Treat the cause: control blood sugar, replace a deficiency, stop a causative medicine or relieve compression. Prescription medicines can ease neuropathic pain, and foot care protects numb feet.
