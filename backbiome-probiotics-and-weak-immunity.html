Probiotics And A Weak Immune System

Probiotics are safe for most healthy adults, with exceptions.*

Take Care If You

  • Have a weakened immune system, including after a transplant or during chemotherapy
  • Have a central venous catheter or a serious illness
  • Have short bowel syndrome or a damaged gut lining

See also: Pregnancy, teens, conditions · Blood thinners and bleeding

Why

In rare cases live bacteria can cause infections in these groups. The strains here are not named, so a doctor cannot easily judge them.

Gas, bloating or loose stools in the first days are common and usually settle.
