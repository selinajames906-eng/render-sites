NuNerve Quick Answers

What is NuNerve?

A liquid dropper supplement (1 fl oz / 30 mL) marketed to support nerve health, with whole coffee fruit extract, PEA, B12, B6 and B1, R-alpha lipoic acid, lion's mane and curcumin, per the seller.

What is inside?

Whole coffee fruit extract, PEA, vitamins B12, B6 and B1, R-alpha lipoic acid, lion's mane mushroom and curcumin, per the seller.

Are the amounts listed?

No. No amount is given, and we were not shown a Supplement Facts panel or a serving size.

Does it treat neuropathy or regrow nerves?

No. It does not treat or reverse neuropathy or nerve damage, and no study of the liquid is shown.

Why does tingling need a doctor first?

It can come from diabetes, B12 deficiency, nerve compression, alcohol, medicines and other causes that have their own treatments.

Does "nano" mean better absorption?

We found no evidence of that for these ingredients.

Is it FDA approved?

No. Supplements are not. The seller only says it is made in an FDA-registered facility.

Who should ask a doctor first?

Anyone with diabetes or on blood thinners, taking other B vitamins, with a mushroom or coffee allergy, gallbladder problems, pregnant, nursing, or under 18.

What does it cost?

$69, $59 or $49 a bottle for 2, 3 or 6 bottles: $138 plus shipping, $177 or $294.

Is it a subscription?

The seller's order page says a one-time fee with no autoship; confirm at checkout.

What is the guarantee?

60 days on the cards and in the text, though one banner says 180 days, so confirm before you buy.

When should I see a doctor?

For any new or persistent tingling, numbness or weakness, and at once for sudden one-sided weakness or a drooping face.
