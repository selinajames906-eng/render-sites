The Sixty-Day Refund Steps

The seller advertises a 60-day, 100% money-back guarantee. Its text says to write to it at any time in the next 60 days for a refund, and does not mention returning the bottles. A banner elsewhere on its page says 180 days.

The Inconsistency

One banner says "180 day money back guarantee", while the guarantee section, the pricing cards and the seal all say 60 days. Count on 60, and ask the seller in writing whether the longer period applies.
