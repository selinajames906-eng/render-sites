How Synevra UltraLift Works and How To Use It: Patch Test First

What the serum does, how to apply it, and what to expect.

What the Serum Does

The serum combines a cosmetic peptide, a hydrating salt of hyaluronic acid, two vitamins and glycerin, so it helps skin hold water and look smoother and brighter for a while.* The seller's "dual-action Lift and Lock" story is marketing, and no study of the finished kit is shown. See the ingredients and the benefits.

Four Steps

Follow these with the daily routine beside you.

1

Patch test

Apply a small amount behind the ear or on the inner forearm and wait 24 hours.

2

Cleanse

Start with clean, slightly damp skin.

3

Apply the serum

Use the amount on your packaging, morning or evening, and let it absorb.

4

Moisturise and protect

Follow with moisturiser, and sunscreen in the morning.

Tips

Small habits make it stick. See daily habits for healthy-looking skin.

  • Keep it away from your eyes.
  • Introduce one new product at a time.
  • Stop using it if skin stings, burns or swells.
  • Store it as the packaging directs.
