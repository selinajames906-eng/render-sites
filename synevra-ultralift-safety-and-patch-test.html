Synevra UltraLift Safety and Patch Test: Who Should Be Careful

A frank look at how to try it safely.

Patch Test First

Apply a small amount behind the ear or on the inner forearm, wait 24 hours and check for redness, itching or swelling. See how to patch test a new serum.

Sensitive and Reactive Skin

Vitamin C and fragrance-free formulas can still sting. Introduce it slowly. See vitamin C and vitamin E.

Keep It Away From Your Eyes

Rinse with water if it gets in, and stop using it if irritation continues. See the FAQ.

Biotin and Blood Tests

Biotin can distort some tests, including thyroid and heart tests, so tell anyone who orders blood work. See biotin and biotin supplements and lab test interference.

The Capsule

The VitaLock Complex lists unnamed botanical extracts and no amounts, and zinc and niacinamide have limits too. See VitaLock Complex.

Sun Protection

Use sunscreen every morning. See sun protection: the biggest anti-aging habit.

Pregnancy, Nursing and Under 18

Ask a doctor first if you are pregnant or nursing, and it is not for anyone under 18. See the FAQ.

When To See a Doctor

Persistent rash, spreading redness, a mole that changes or skin that does not heal needs a doctor, not a serum. See how to spot anti-aging skincare hype and the before you order checklist.
